# General Refund Policy

Placeholder policy content for this hobby-scale project (spec-2-1's Ask
First: fabricated, not the store's real legal policy). Ingested clause by
clause by `scripts/ingest_policy_docs.py`; each `## N. Title` heading below
becomes one citable Policy Store chunk.

## 1. Return Window

Customers may request a refund within 30 days of the order's completion
date. Requests submitted after the 30-day window has closed are not
eligible for a refund under this policy, regardless of reason, unless the
item qualifies under Section 3 of `restocking-and-exceptions` (damaged or
defective items).

## 2. Proof of Purchase

A valid order reference number is required to process any refund request.
The order must exist in the store's records and must be associated with a
completed transaction. Refund requests that cannot be matched to a
completed order are not eligible for a refund under this policy.

## 3. Refund Amount

The refunded amount may not exceed the original order's total amount. A
customer may request a partial refund for less than the full order amount;
a request for more than the order's amount is not eligible for a refund
under this policy. If no specific amount is stated in the request, the full
order amount is refunded.

## 4. Refund Method

Approved refunds are issued back to the original payment method used for
the order. No cash, store credit, or alternate payment method substitutions
are offered under this policy.
