# Restocking Fees and Non-Returnable Items

Placeholder policy content for this hobby-scale project (spec-2-1's Ask
First: fabricated, not the store's real legal policy). Ingested clause by
clause by `scripts/ingest_policy_docs.py`; each `## N. Title` heading below
becomes one citable Policy Store chunk.

## 1. Non-Returnable Categories

The following item categories are not eligible for a refund under this
policy: digital downloads and gift cards, once delivered or redeemed;
perishable goods; and personal care items that have been opened or used.
An order consisting entirely of non-returnable items is not eligible for a
refund under this policy.

## 2. Restocking Fee

Returns of opened electronics and large appliances are subject to a 15%
restocking fee, deducted from the refunded amount. The restocking fee does
not apply to items returned unopened and in their original packaging, or to
items covered by Section 3 below.

## 3. Damaged or Defective Items

Items that arrived damaged or are defective are exempt from the
restocking fee in Section 2 and from the non-returnable category
restrictions in Section 1 -- a damaged or defective item is always eligible
for a full refund of its purchase price, regardless of category or
condition on return.
